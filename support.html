FAQ

Why does recording stop when I switch apps or lock the phone?
iOS allows an app to use the camera only while it is on screen. DashRoute saves the open segment, and when you come back within 5 minutes the same trip continues. Use the dim-screen mode instead of locking the phone.
How do I make recording start automatically in the car?
Settings → Auto-start walks you through a Shortcuts automation: when your iPhone connects to the car's Bluetooth or CarPlay, DashRoute opens and starts recording. You set it up once.
What is protected, exactly?
When a hard brake, impact, bump or possible crash is detected, or when you mark a moment, the 60 seconds before and after are locked. Loop recording never deletes them. Free keeps the last 10 protected events; Pro keeps all of them.
The app says the phone is hot.
Quality drops to 720p and recording continues; if the phone gets critically hot, recording stops to protect it. Keep the phone out of direct sun, use the dim-screen mode and avoid charging through a hot cable.
Where are my videos? Can I get them onto my Mac?
Open a trip, make a clip and save it to Photos or export it to Files; from there AirDrop or iCloud Drive works as usual. Raw loop video stays inside the app.
Plate blur missed a plate.
Plate blur uses on-device text recognition and works best on sharp, well-lit plates. Check the preview before sharing; you can also choose a different clip window.
I bought Pro on another iPhone.
Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.
How do I cancel?
iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → DashRoute. Cancelling keeps Pro until the end of the paid period. The lifetime purchase is a one-time payment and never renews, so there is nothing to cancel.
How do I delete everything?
Delete trips from the Trips list, or delete the app: your videos, routes and events are not stored anywhere else. Usage statistics and purchase records (tied to a random ID, never your name or email) are described in the Privacy Policy.
Can I turn off usage statistics?
Yes: Settings → Privacy → Usage statistics. DashRoute then sends no usage events; purchase records are still kept by Apple and RevenueCat.
