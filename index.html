DashRoute

Dash cam & trip map for iPhone

Your iPhone is now a dash cam. Brake hard and that moment protects itself: 60 seconds before and after are locked, with the exact spot marked on the map. Scrub the video and the car moves along the route; tap the route and the video jumps there.

  • Loop recording in two modes: keep events only, or keep the whole trip.
  • Hard braking, impacts, bumps and possible crashes detected on the phone.
  • Video and map in sync, searchable trip archive.
  • Clips with speed, location and a route mini map; plate blur and PDF incident report with Pro (monthly, yearly or a one-time lifetime purchase).
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. Video, routes and events stay on your iPhone; usage statistics and purchase records are collected with a random ID, never your name or email.
